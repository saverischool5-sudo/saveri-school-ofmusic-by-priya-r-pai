Welcome to Saveri

Acclaimed musician Priya R Pai leads the Saveri School of Music by Priya R Pai with a vision to preserve and propagate the rich heritage of Carnatic music.

Joined by her talented daughters Shradha R Pai and Shreya R Pai, the institution stands as a beacon of premier music education in Kochi, blending traditional wisdom with modern teaching methodologies for over 30 years.

Read Our Story

Meet The Trio

Saveri School of Music by Priya R Pai is uniquely led by Priya R Pai alongside her two daughters, Shradha R Pai and Shreya R Pai. All three are not just dedicated teachers, but active performers.

Together, this dynamic trio frequently performs spectacular Classical Fusion and Carnatic Vocal Concerts, bringing decades of rich tradition and vibrant new energy to stages across the country.
