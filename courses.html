Courses

Dedicated training across Carnatic music and devotional disciplines, rooted in the Guru-Shishya Parampara.

Classical Carnatic Music

Rooted in ancient tradition, our core vocal curriculum is meticulously structured into three comprehensive tiers — guiding students from their very first notes to advanced concert-level repertoire.

  • ♪Carnatic Music (Basics): Foundation in Swara varisai, Alankaras, Geethams, and Swarajathis.
  • ♪Carnatic Music (Medium Level): Introduction to Varnams, simple Kritis, and complex Talas.
  • ♪Carnatic Music (Advanced): Mastery of Manodharma improvisation, Ragam Tanam Pallavi (RTP), and advanced compositions.

Sacred Sahasranamams

The chanting of Sahasranamams — a thousand names of the divine — is a deeply spiritual practice that purifies the mind and environment. We emphasize perfect Sanskrit pronunciation, breath control, and proper intonation.

  • ♪Lalithasahasranamam: The thousand names of Devi, chanted for spiritual awakening and prosperity.
  • ♪Vishnu Sahasranamam: The sacred thousand names of Lord Vishnu, celebrated for its universal peace-giving vibrations.

Classical Literature

Immerse yourself in the poetic and spiritual masterpieces of ancient Indian literature through song. These works combine profound philosophy with exquisite musical framing.

  • ♪Ashtapadi: Jayadeva's masterpiece exploring the divine love of Radha and Krishna through Sanskrit verses.
  • ♪Narayaneeyam: Melpathur Narayana Bhattathiri's majestic summary of the Bhagavata Purana.
  • ♪Soundaryalahari: Adi Shankaracharya's breathtaking work praising the beauty and power of Goddess Parvati.
Inquire About Admissions

Devotional & Light Music

Soulful musical training designed for everyday prayer, spiritual gatherings, and lighter classical performances. Perfect for looks seeking a gentle and heartfelt entry into the world of Indian music.

  • ♪Bhajans: Energetic and group-oriented devotional songs in various Indian languages.
  • ♪Light Music: Lighter classical compositions, Abhangs, and semi-classical melodies.
  • ♪Prayer Songs: Essential daily prayers and short invocations for all traditions.
