Meet The Founder

Saveri School of Music by Priya R Pai was established 30 years ago by Priya R Pai, an acclaimed classical musician, playback singer, and prolific composer. With a monumental portfolio of over 500 albums and 300 original compositions, her vision is to create a sanctuary where the ancient sounds of Carnatic music are preserved and celebrated.

She is joined by her talented daughters, Shradha R Pai and Shreya R Pai, who are also dedicated teachers at the institution. Together, the trio frequently performs captivating classical fusion and Carnatic vocal concerts.

Saveri School is deeply committed to giving students real-world exposure. Priya R Pai and her daughters actively conduct musical programs and record professional albums alongside their students, while also hosting annual music workshops across Kerala and outside the state.

A Legacy of Composition

Priya R Pai is celebrated not only as an exceptional teacher, but as a master composer and playback singer. Over her illustrious 30-year career, she has released over 500 music albums and composed more than 300 original compositions.

Her works span across Carnatic classical, light music, and devotional genres, touching the hearts of thousands. This immense creative output continues to inspire her students, who are frequently invited to collaborate on these recordings and gain unparalleled industry experience.

Through her compositions, Priya R Pai ensures that the rich heritage of Indian classical music is continually evolving and reaching new generations of listeners across the globe.
